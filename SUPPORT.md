## Questions

Use GitHub Discussions after it is enabled for setup, architecture, Digital Twin, motion-control, and development questions.

Include:

- operating system;
- .NET SDK version;
- Digital Twin version when relevant;
- machine profile;
- command used;
- relevant logs.

## Defects

Use the bug-report form for reproducible incorrect behavior.

## Feature proposals

Use the engineering-change form. Explain the requirement and why the current architecture is insufficient.

## Sensitive security or unsafe-motion reports

Do not open a public issue. Follow `SECURITY.md`.

Support is community-based and does not provide certified machinery-safety or production-commissioning services.
